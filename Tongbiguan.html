BEST-Tongbiguan




Mt. Tongbiguan (铜壁关)

Mt. Tongbiguan is located in Tongbiguan Provincial Nature Reserve (97°31′-97°46′E, 23°54′-24°51′N), southwest Yunnan, China. The reserve spans Yingjiang, Longchuan and Ruili counties(cities) of Dehong Dai-Jingpo Autonomous Prefecture, bordering Myanmar. It has a tropical monsoon climate influenced by the Indian Ocean warm current, with distinct dry-wet seasons, abundant heat and plentiful rainfall. The annual mean temperature is 19.47 ℃, annual precipitation ranges from 1402.2-1664.7 mm, and precipitation from May to October accounts for 89% of the total annual rainfall.

Vegetation and Flora

Tongbiguan Provincial Nature Reserve hosts typical tropical seasonal rain forest dominated by Dipterocarpus retusus, Vatica xishuangbannaensis and Shorea assamica, together with tropical low-montane evergreen broad-leaved forest and monsoon evergreen broad-leaved forest.

There are 5006 vascular plant species recorded in the reserve. Among them, 9 species are classified as China’s first-class protected plants, and 137 species belong to the second-class protected plants.

Fauna

A total of 725 animal species have been recorded in the nature reserve. Fifteen vertebrate species belong to the first-class protected animals of China, including Hoolock tianxing, Viverra zibetha, Neofelis nebulosa and Polyplectron bicalcaratum. Seventy-four species are China’s second-class protected animals.

Research sites of BEST network

In 2024, 18 plots (SL79-SL96, 50 m × 50 m each) were newly established in Nabang area of Dehong. These plots cover Dipterocarpus retusus forest, Vatica xishuangbannaensis forest, Shorea yunnanensis forest, tropical low-montane evergreen broad-leaved forest and monsoon evergreen broad-leaved forest.
