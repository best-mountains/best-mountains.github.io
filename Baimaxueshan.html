BEST-Baimaxueshan




Mt. Baimaxueshan (白马雪山)

Mt. Baimaxueshan is located in Baimaxueshan National Nature Reserve (98°55′-99°24′E, 27°24′-28°35′N), Deqin and Weixi counties, Diqing Tibetan Autonomous Prefecture, northwest Yunnan, China. It lies in the middle Hengduan Mountains on the southern extension of the Qinghai-Tibet Plateau. The reserve features prominent vertical mountain climate. It belongs to the cold-temperate mountain monsoon climate, with distinct dry-wet seasons, long cold winter and short summer. The average annual temperature is 4.7 ℃, the annual precipitation ranges from 633.7-850 mm, and the mean relative humidity is 71%.

Vegetation and Flora

Baimaxueshan National Nature Reserve has complete mountain vertical vegetation spectra, containing 7 vegetation zones, 11 vegetation subtypes and 37 formations, dominated by alpine dark coniferous forests.

A total of 2343 vascular plant species, belonging to 687 genera and 156 families, have been recorded in the reserve. Two plant species belong to the first-class protected plants of China, and 24 species belong to the second-class protected plants of China.

Fauna

There are 521 vertebrate species and 447 insect species recorded in the nature reserve. Eleven vertebrate species belong to the first-class protected animals of China, including Rhinopithecus bieti, Panthera uncia, Neofelis nebulosa and so on.

Research sites of BEST network

Baimaxueshan elevational gradient series (SL29-SL34, 6 plots): continuous elevational gradient from 3200 m (Pinus armandii forest) →3400 m (Pinus densata forest / conifer-broad-leaved mixed forest) →3600 m (Quercus aquifolioides forest) →3800 m (Abies georgei forest) →4000 m (Larix potaninii var. macrocarpa forest). Each plot is 100 m × 100 m.
